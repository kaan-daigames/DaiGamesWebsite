Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account to play?

No. You can play the whole game without signing in, and your progress stays on your device. An account is only needed if you want your times on the online leaderboard.

How do I delete my account?

Open the game and go to Settings › DELETE ACCOUNT. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, your leaderboard times and the progress stored on your device. If you can't open the game, email us and we'll delete it for you.

How do I keep my times when I change phones?

Sign in with the same Apple account on your new device. Your best times are restored from the leaderboard. Progress from playing without an account is stored only on the old device and can't be transferred.

Can I change my leaderboard nickname?

Your nickname is shown publicly next to your times. If you need it changed or removed, email us and include the nickname so we can find your account.

I found a bug. How do I report it?

Thank you! Email us with the details listed on this page. Screenshots or a short screen recording help a lot.

What information do you collect?

As little as possible. Read the full details in our Privacy Policy.
