About Us

Dai Games is a mobile gaming company. We create immersive and fun games for the phone in your pocket.

We believe a great mobile game should be easy to pick up, hard to put down, and leave you feeling better than before you started. That means worlds worth getting lost in, controls that feel natural under your thumbs, and moments of delight you want to share.

As a focused, independent gaming company, we give every title our full attention: carefully designed, thoroughly tested, and respectful of players' time and privacy.

  • Immersive

    Worlds, sound and game feel that draw players in, even on a small screen.

  • Fun

    Play that's bright, generous and rewarding to come back to.

  • Made for mobile

    Designed for touch from day one, with privacy and performance built in.
