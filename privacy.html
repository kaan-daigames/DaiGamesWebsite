Echo Protocol – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Echo Protocol (“the game”) is a puzzle game for iPhone, iPad and Android made by Dai Games, Inc. This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and how you can delete it.

The short version: you can play the whole game without signing in. The game gives every player a guest account, a random ID without your name, email or contact details, so finished times can be checked for cheating. The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which receives device information to show and measure ads; you can choose whether ads are personalised. Optional in-app purchases are paid through Apple or Google Play, and we never see your payment details. Signing in is only needed for the online leaderboard, and you can delete that account at any time from inside the game.

1. Playing without signing in

Your best times, stars, completed tutorials and sound settings are stored on your device and removed when you delete the game. Items you buy are unlocked on your device and can be restored from the store (section 4).

The first time the game starts, it creates a guest account on our server. It is a random ID that doesn't include your name, email or contact details, and the server's login tokens for it are kept on your device. The game uses it to check finished times for cheating, for every player, signed in or not:

  • When you start a level, the game tells the server which level and whether you play on iPhone/iPad or Android, and the server returns an ID for that attempt.
  • When you finish, the game sends your time with that ID.
  • If you watch a rewarded ad during a level, the game tells the server when it opened and closed and whether you got the reward, so the time spent in the ad isn't counted against you. Google's ad service also confirms to our server that the ad was watched to the end, using an ID the game creates for that ad view.
  • On iPhone and iPad, the game also creates a security key on your device with Apple's App Attest, so the server can confirm the time came from the real game. The key doesn't include your name or contact details.

A guest's times are not shown on the public leaderboard. If you later sign in (section 2), your guest times move to your account. The ads in the game work the same either way; see section 3 for what Google receives.

When you sign in, Apple or Google gives the game a short-lived sign-in token. The game sends it once to our server to confirm your sign-in, and the server then gives the game its own login tokens. These tokens are kept on your device so you stay signed in.

3. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads from Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between some levels, a banner at the bottom of the leaderboard, and rewarded ads that only play when you choose them (Revive, Heal and Extra Bullet).

To show and measure ads, Google's ad software in the game receives information such as your IP address, device model, operating system and app version, an approximate location worked out from your IP address, how you interact with ads, and diagnostic data. On iPhone and iPad, it only receives your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) if you allow tracking. On Android, it receives your device's advertising ID unless you have deleted it in your Android settings.

You decide how your information is used for ads:

  • Europe (EEA, UK and Switzerland): before any ads are shown, Google's consent form asks whether your information may be used for personalised ads. You can change your answer at any time in Settings › Privacy Choices in the game.
  • United States: under some US state privacy laws (such as California's), letting Google use your information for personalised ads counts as "sharing" it. You can opt out at any time in Settings › Privacy Choices in the game.
  • Apple's tracking popup (iPhone and iPad): the game asks whether it may track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you say no, the IDFA is not shared. You can change this at any time in iPhone or iPad Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • Android advertising ID: you can delete or reset it at any time in your Android settings, usually under Settings › Privacy › Ads or Settings › Google › Ads. Once deleted, it is not shared.

If you decline, the game still shows ads, but they are not personalised.

If you buy Remove Ads, the Premium Bundle or the All-In-One Bundle, the game shows no ads at all, including the leaderboard banner, and you get rewarded-ad rewards (Revive, Heal and Extra Bullet) without watching an ad (section 4).

We don't receive any personal information about you from Google. Apart from the rewarded-ad confirmation in section 1, we only receive total numbers, such as how many ads were shown.

See Google's Privacy Policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google's ad settings.

4. In-app purchases

The game sells optional one-time unlocks, such as Remove Ads, skins, cosmetics and bundles. There are no subscriptions.

Every purchase is paid through Apple's App Store on iPhone or iPad, or Google Play on Android. Apple or Google processes the payment, and we never receive your card details, billing address or Apple or Google Account information.

To unlock what you bought, the game receives transaction details from Apple or Google, such as the product, a transaction ID and the purchase date. These are checked and kept on your device. The game uses Unity's In-App Purchasing software to talk to the store, so Unity may process these transaction details too; see Unity's Privacy Policy.

If you are signed in, the leaderboard look you chose (nickname colour, frame and supporter badge) is sent to our server so it can be shown on the leaderboard (section 2).

Your purchases belong to your Apple or Google Account, so you can get them back on a new device or after reinstalling. On iPhone and iPad, use Restore Purchases in the game; on Android, they are restored automatically when the game starts.

Refunds are handled by the store: Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, and Google Play through Google Play's refund process.

5. What we don't do

  • No ad tracking on Apple devices unless you allow it in Apple's tracking popup.
  • No selling or renting of your information. The only "sharing" is the ad information Google receives (section 3), which US users can opt out of.
  • No third-party analytics tools; Google AdMob (section 3) is the only third party that receives data for ads.
  • No access to your payment information; Apple and Google Play handle all payments (section 4).

6. Where the information is stored

Account information (the guest account ID, its level attempts and times, and once you sign in your Apple user ID or Google account ID, email, nickname, best times and leaderboard look) is stored on the game's server, which is hosted by a cloud hosting provider. All communication between the game and the server is encrypted with HTTPS.

Ad information (section 3) is collected and processed by Google under its own privacy policy, not on the game's server.

The game is built with the Unity engine. Unity may receive basic, anonymous technical information about the device (such as the device model and operating system version) to keep the engine working well. This information does not identify you. See Unity's Privacy Policy.

Sign in with Apple and the App Store are provided by Apple. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Google sign-in and Google Play are provided by Google. See Google's Privacy Policy.

7. How long information is kept

Your account information is kept for as long as your account exists. If you sign out, your account stays on the server so you can sign in again later; your progress on the device is kept too, and the game starts a new guest account.

Guest accounts that haven't been used for 12 months are deleted automatically, with their times. If you delete the game without signing in, the device loses its link to the guest account, which doesn't include your name or contact details, and it is deleted after those 12 months.

Ad information is kept by Google according to its own retention rules.

8. Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time inside the game: Settings › DELETE ACCOUNT.

This permanently deletes, right away:

  • your Apple user ID or Google account ID, email, nickname, leaderboard look and all your times from the server and the leaderboard;
  • on iPhone and iPad, the game's link to your Apple sign-in (it is revoked with Apple);
  • the game's security key for your account and your level progress (stars and best times) stored on your device.

Your settings, tutorial hints, privacy choices and purchases stay on the device.

You can also stop the game's use of Sign in with Apple from your iPhone or iPad: Settings › [your name] › Sign-In & Security › Sign in with Apple › Echo Protocol › Stop Using Apple ID. The game then signs you out the next time it starts.

On Android, you can also remove the game's access to your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/connections.

Deleting your account does not cancel or refund your purchases. They stay with your Apple or Google Account and can be restored (section 4).

If you can't open the game, email us (address below) and we will delete your account by hand.

If you never signed in, there is no Delete Account button. Guest accounts aren't linked to your name or contact details, and the game doesn't show the guest ID, so we can't tell which guest account is yours and can't delete one on request. Guest accounts are deleted automatically after 12 months without use (section 7). If you sign in, your guest times move to your account and are deleted with it.

Deleting your account does not affect information Google holds for ads; use Settings › Privacy Choices, Apple's tracking setting, your Android advertising ID setting or Google's own tools for that.

9. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. Without signing in we receive no name, email or contact details (the guest account is only a random ID, see section 1), though Google AdMob still receives device information to show ads (section 3). If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we will delete it. Parents can control purchases with Apple's Screen Time and Ask to Buy settings, or with Google Play's purchase approvals and Family Link.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your information, and to object to its use. You can delete everything yourself from the game (if you signed in), or email us for any other request. We will answer within 30 days. For information used for ads, use Settings › Privacy Choices, Apple's tracking setting, your Android advertising ID setting, or contact Google.

11. Changes to this policy
